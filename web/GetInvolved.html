Donate

Your financial contributions are vital to the success of our programs. Donations go directly towards funding our public health programming projects, community development programs, and Refugee Youth Education in Rwanda.

Volunteer Talent Pool

Engineers4Humanity welcomes dedicated professionals and volunteers across multiple fields — engineering, environmental studies, business development, communications, design, and community engagement. Submit your résumé to join our Volunteer Talent Pool. Our team will contact you when your skills align with current program needs in Rwanda.

Professional & Institutional Partnership

Organizations seeking to create sustainable impact are invited to partner with us. We collaborate with engineering companies, academic institutions, youth organizations, peace building groups, and humanitarian agencies to empower refugees and strengthen community resilience. Let's work together to build hope, opportunity, and long term self reliance of refugees in Rwanda.
